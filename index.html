Privacy Policy

CarBrain: OBD2 ELM327 Scanner
Developer: Mohamed Elsayed · Effective: 16 November 2026

This policy covers the CarBrain app, diagnostic assistance, support and this website. Processing depends on the features you choose and the app version you use. Describing an optional feature does not mean every release enables it.

We use relevant information to provide vehicle diagnostics and assistance. Analysis can involve sending data to service providers, even when permanent storage is not the purpose. We do not promise that providers keep no logs or that all processing has zero retention.

1. Information processed

  • Vehicle diagnostics: vehicle details, fault codes, supported sensor readings, scan results, symptoms and diagnostic history. A VIN or other identifier may be present in vehicle records; avoid adding identifiers to free text unless necessary.
  • Records: maintenance, fuel, mileage, document and reminder entries that you add. Receipt text recognition is described in the app as on-device processing. Exporting or sharing a document is a separate action.
  • Location: with your device permission, location can support nearby fuel searches and directions. Mapping and navigation providers handle their requests under their own policies. Location permission does not authorize unrelated AI analysis.
  • Support: your email address, message and attachments that you choose to send. Remove unnecessary VINs, plates, addresses and payment details before sending screenshots or logs.
  • Technical data: online services receive connection information such as IP address and request time and may process security, error and operational logs.

2. Local data and online diagnostic analysis

Core scanning and manual records do not require an account. Local records are distinct from data sent for online analysis. Device backups and files you export may also contain your records and are governed by your chosen services.

When online AI analysis is available and you choose to request it, relevant codes, readings, vehicle context and symptoms can be sent to CarBrain's backend and an AI provider to generate troubleshooting guidance. The planned service uses Render for backend hosting and OpenAI's API for analysis. Availability depends on the release and service configuration. You should receive an explanation and a choice before information is shared with an AI provider.

Do not include names, contact details, exact location, full VINs or document images in an AI request unless the feature explicitly needs them. Permission to generate a diagnostic response does not by itself authorize unrelated advertising or model training.

3. Purposes and sharing

Information is used to provide requested features, interpret vehicle data, generate diagnostic guidance, maintain chosen records, answer support requests, troubleshoot failures, protect the service and comply with legal obligations. Permanent collection for its own sake is not the purpose. CarBrain does not sell diagnostic data or use it for targeted advertising.

Relevant data may be processed by hosting and AI providers for online requests, mapping/navigation providers for nearby services and directions, and email providers for support. Processing can occur outside your country. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, security or prevent misuse.

Provider information: Render privacy, OpenAI API data controls, and Apple privacy. OpenAI API retention depends on the endpoint and account configuration. Its documentation describes default abuse-monitoring retention of up to 30 days, subject to exceptions; some features also retain application state. Do not assume zero retention.

4. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them through available app controls or remove the app's local data. Backups and exported copies can remain separately. Online request data and technical logs may be retained as needed to provide the requested service, investigate errors, maintain security or meet legal obligations. Provider retention may differ from our own. Immediate deletion from all logs and backups is not guaranteed.

For information sent to support or an online service, email us to request access, correction or deletion. We assess requests, verify only what is reasonably necessary and explain applicable limits. Please do not send identity documents with your initial request.

5. Choices and rights

You may decline optional online analysis, manage Bluetooth, location, camera, photo and notification permissions in iPhone Settings and choose which records to share. Features needing a denied permission may be unavailable. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing, object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a competent privacy authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

6. Security and children

We seek appropriate safeguards and limit information to what the requested function needs. No device, network or service is completely secure. CarBrain is intended for people able to use vehicle diagnostic tools responsibly and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child supplied personal information.

7. Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site code adds no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. An email link opens your email app; no message is sent until you send it.

8. Changes and contact

We update this policy when practices change and revise the effective date. Materially different uses require appropriate notice and, where applicable, consent before they begin.

المعلومات والأغراض

قد يعالج التطبيق بيانات السيارة والأكواد والقراءات ونتائج الفحص والأعراض والسجل التشخيصي، وسجلات الصيانة والوقود والمستندات والتذكيرات التي تضيفها. قد تحتوي السجلات على رقم الهيكل VIN. قراءة نص الإيصالات موصوفة في التطبيق بأنها تتم على الجهاز؛ تصدير المستند أو مشاركته إجراء منفصل.

يُستخدم الموقع بإذن الجهاز للعثور على محطات الوقود والاتجاهات، وتخضع طلبات الخرائط والملاحة لسياسات مزوّديها. إذن الموقع لا يجيز إرساله لتحليل ذكاء اصطناعي غير متعلق به. نتلقى البريد والرسالة والمرفقات التي ترسلها للدعم. تتلقى الخدمات المتصلة عنوان IP ووقت الطلب وقد تحفظ سجلات الأمان والأخطاء والتشغيل.

تُستخدم المعلومات لتقديم الميزات المطلوبة وتفسير البيانات وإرشادات التشخيص والسجلات المختارة والدعم ومعالجة الأعطال وحماية الخدمة والقانون. لا يبيع CarBrain بيانات التشخيص ولا يستخدمها للإعلانات الموجّهة. جمع البيانات للاحتفاظ الدائم بها لذاته ليس الهدف.

المعالجة المحلية والذكاء الاصطناعي

لا تتطلب الميزات الأساسية للفحص والسجلات اليدوية حسابًا. البيانات المحلية تختلف عن المعلومات المرسلة للتحليل؛ وقد تشمل نسخ الجهاز الاحتياطية والملفات المصدّرة سجلاتك. عند إتاحة التحليل عبر الإنترنت واختيارك طلبه، يمكن إرسال الأكواد والقراءات وسياق السيارة والأعراض ذات الصلة إلى خادم CarBrain ومزوّد ذكاء اصطناعي. تستخدم الخدمة المخططة Render للاستضافة وواجهة OpenAI API للتحليل، حسب الإصدار وإعداد الخدمة. ينبغي توضيح المشاركة وإتاحة الاختيار قبل إرسال المعلومات لمزوّد الذكاء الاصطناعي.

لا تُدرج أسماء أو بيانات اتصال أو موقعًا دقيقًا أو رقم هيكل كاملًا أو صور مستندات إلا إذا تطلبتها الميزة صراحة. إذن تحليل الفحص لا يعني الإذن بالإعلانات أو تدريب النماذج لأغراض أخرى.

المشاركة والاحتفاظ والحذف

قد يعالج مزوّدو الاستضافة والذكاء الاصطناعي والخرائط والبريد البيانات ذات الصلة داخل بلدك أو خارجه. قد نُفصح المعلومات للالتزام بالقانون أو حماية الحقوق والأمان ومنع إساءة الاستخدام. روابط سياسات المزوّدين مذكورة أعلاه.

تظل السجلات المحلية حتى حذفها بالأدوات المتاحة أو إزالة بيانات التطبيق؛ وقد تبقى النسخ الاحتياطية والمصدّرة منفصلة. قد تُحفظ الطلبات والسجلات التقنية بقدر ما يلزم للخدمة والأخطاء والأمان والقانون. تختلف مدد المزوّدين ولا نضمن انعدام الاحتفاظ أو الحذف الفوري من كل السجلات والنسخ. تصف وثائق OpenAI احتفاظًا افتراضيًا بسجلات مراقبة إساءة الاستخدام يصل إلى 30 يومًا مع استثناءات، وقد تحتفظ بعض الميزات بحالة التطبيق.

Connection checklist

  1. Park safely. Use a compatible Bluetooth Low Energy ELM327 OBD-II adapter and supported vehicle. Classic Bluetooth-only adapters may not work with iPhone.
  2. Follow the vehicle/adapter instructions, turn the ignition on when appropriate and disconnect other scanning apps.
  3. Allow Bluetooth access. In CarBrain, use Diagnose → Connect ELM, then Scan My Car.
  4. Check scan coverage: unsupported or missing responses do not prove there are no faults.

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, CarBrain version, adapter model and a short description. Remove private information from screenshots and logs. Do not send passwords, API keys or payment details.

CarBrain does not establish whether a vehicle is safe to drive or replace inspection by a qualified professional. Use diagnostic controls while parked.
